# Prompt: Create a social campaign creative system

Use WizardSkill and inspect any supplied brand assets before creating a visual direction.

Campaign details:
- Brand: [brand]
- Audience: [audience]
- Platform and format: [platform / dimensions]
- Objective: [awareness / engagement / leads / sales]
- Offer and CTA: [offer / action]
- Verified claims and proof: [facts]
- Brand rules: [colors / logo / fonts / restrictions]

Create an original creative direction with:
1. A single strong message and visual hook.
2. A clear hierarchy for hook, supporting detail, brand, and CTA.
3. Legible typography and mobile-safe spacing.
4. Platform-appropriate dimensions and safe zones.
5. Optional variants that test distinct hooks or compositions.
6. A short rationale and export checklist.

Do not invent statistics, testimonials, endorsements, or product capabilities. Confirm licensing for assets and fonts. Keep brand identity consistent across variants.
